## SCHEDULE 2 — SITE ZONE FACTORS AND WORK AREAS

The factors below apply to every item in Series A, B, C and D. They do not apply to Series E, which is measured at the base rate in every zone.

| Zone             | Description                                     |   Factor |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Z1 Compound      | Within 500 m of the site compound               |        1 |
| Z2 North Spur    | North spur, 500 m to 2 km from the compound     |     1.06 |
| Z3 Wadi Crossing | Wadi crossing and approaches, restricted access |    1.145 |
| Z4 Escarpment    | Escarpment platform, single-track haul only     |     1.28 |
