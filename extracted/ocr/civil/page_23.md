## SCHEDULE 3 — GROUND CLASSIFICATION FACTORS

The factors below apply only to the items listed beneath the table. An item not listed is measured without regard to the ground classification (Clause 29).

| Class             | Description                                          |   Factor |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| G1 Loose Sand     | Loose windblown sand, no excavation support required |     0.94 |
| G2 Firm Sabkha    | Firm sabkha, the datum classification                |        1 |
| G3 CementedFill   | Cemented fill and demolition arisings                |     1.12 |
| G4 Weathered Rock | Weathered rock, rippable by tracked plant            |    1.375 |
| G5 Sound Rock     | Sound rock requiring breaking or hydraulic splitting |     1.63 |
