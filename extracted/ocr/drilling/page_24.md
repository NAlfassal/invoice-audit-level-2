## SCHEDULE 5 — THE DAILY DRILLING REPORT AND ITS PARTS

One Daily Drilling Report is issued for each well and day (Clause 19). It carries the operations summary and, as numbered parts, evéry other record this Contract makes a condition of payment. There are no separate run, survey, source or loss documents.

|    | Part Contents                                                                                                                                                          | Completed                         |
|----|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| A  | Operations summary: section, status, depths, circulating hours, BHA run number, tools in hole, personnel on the rig, and the quantities measured that day              | every day                         |
| B  | BHA run record: run number, first and last day of the run, tools in the run, circulating hours, metres logged and reamed, and whether a radioactive source was carried | every day of a run                |
| C  | Gyro survey record: surveys taken that day                                                                                                                             | a day on which surveys were taken |
| .D | Radioactive source handling: the sources handled and their certification a day on which a source                                                                       | was run                           |
| E  | Lost in hole: the tool, its service code, the run, and the circulating hours a day on which a tool accumulated on the well at the time of the loss                     | was lost                          |

A charge for a service below is not payable until the part named has been completed in the Daily Drilling Report for the day or run the charge is for (Clause 37). A part that does not apply to the day is omitted; a part that applies and is absent is a record not delivered.
