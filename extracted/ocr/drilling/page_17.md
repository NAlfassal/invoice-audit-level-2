## SCHEDULE 2 — PERFORMANCE DRILLING DEPTH BANDS

Item PD-210 is charged at the rate for the band in which the metres lie (Clause 23). A boundary depth belongs to the shallower band.

| Band   | Measured depth          |   Rate per metre (USD) |
|--------|-------------------------|------------------------|
| Band 1 | 0 m to 1,500 m          |                  42.35 |
| Band 2 | over 1,500 m to 3,000 m |                  58.15 |
| Band 3 | over 3,000 m to 4,500 m |                  76.45 |
| Band 4 | over 4,500 m            |                  98.70 |

The rate is class-rated under Schedule 3 Part 2 and is not section-rated.

## Part 2 — Metres drilled in the Contract Year

The rate for item PD-210 is taken at the percentage below according to the metres already drilled on the well in the Contract Year (Clause 3A). Metres are counted from zero at the start of each Contract Year, and an interval that crosses a band is divided at the band.

| Band   | Metres in the Contract Year   | Per cent of rate   |
|--------|-------------------------------|--------------------|
| Band 1 | 1 m to 40,000 m               | 100%               |
| Band 2 | 40,001 m to 120,000 m         | 96%                |
| Band 3 | above 120,000 m               | 92%                |
