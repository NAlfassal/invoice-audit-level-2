# Invoice audit guidelines — DDS-2025-118

For invoices under *Directional drilling services*. The contract governs; these are the checks to make against it, in order.

1. **Work date first.** Which term, which rate, which discount -- all of it follows the date the work was done, not the date on the invoice.
2. **The record is the evidence.** A charge is worth what the site paperwork supports; without a record it is unevidenced, not cheap.
3. **Say when you do not know.** An invoice you cannot price is a query. Record what is missing rather than guessing a figure.

## The checks

1. **The invoice belongs to this contract** -- reference, issuer and period.
2. **The contract was live on the work dates**, taking the term as extended by every supplement and amendment.
3. **The invoice was raised inside its window**, and after the period it bills for had closed.
4. **Every line has its record** for that day or run, signed by whoever the contract says signs it.
5. **The quantities are the recorded ones.** Billed above the record is payable up to the record, not at all above it.
6. **Each line is identified** against a priced item; where two could fit, leave it unresolved rather than choose on price.
7. **The rate is the one in force on the work date** -- instruments read in the order issued, the later one governing from its effective date.
8. **Adjustments are applied in the contract's order and rounded its way**, with any discount only from the date it started and at the percentage then in force.
9. **Limits hold**: caps, exclusion windows, once-only items and minimum charges.
10. **Nothing is billed twice**, within this invoice or against an earlier one.
11. **The arithmetic reconciles**: line amounts, subtotal, and whatever the contract adds or deducts on the total.
12. **The outcome is written down** -- pass, query, or part-reject -- with the clause relied on, the figure you say is payable, and anything you could not determine.

Where these guidelines and the contract differ, the contract governs — and the difference is worth reporting.
